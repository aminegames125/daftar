## Full description (max 4000)

Daftar is a private notebook for the things you want to keep track of: the places you love, the items you own, and the everyday details that are easy to forget.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Daftar works fully offline, so it is there when your signal is not.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Save places on a map: cafes, shops, clinics, anywhere. Add notes, phone numbers and photos.
- Tap "I'm here" to fill in a place from your current location, and see how far you are from the places you saved.
- Search for an address when you want to add a place from afar.
- Scan a barcode to look up a product, or snap a photo and let your phone read the text on it.
- Keep your notes and lists organized in one tidy place.
- Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
- Share a place link from Google Maps straight into Daftar to create a new entry.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Your data lives only on your device, in a private database.
- Location is used on your phone, only while the app is open, and only if you allow it.
- No servers of ours, nothing to sign up for.

A few optional features use the internet when you use them: Google Maps for the map, OpenStreetMap for address search, and Open Food Facts for barcode lookups. Details are in our privacy policy.

Daftar. Your life, in your pocket.
