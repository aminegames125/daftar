# Daftar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Daftar is a personal organizer that keeps your data on your phone. This page explains, in plain English, what the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

- Everything you enter (places, notes, items, photos, and so on) is stored only on your device.
- We do not run a server. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to see your data.
- We do not sell, share, or collect your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics.

## Data stored on your device

Daftar saves what you create inside the app in a private database on your phone. Other apps cannot read it. If you uninstall Daftar, this data is deleted. Daftar does not back it up to any server run by us.

## Location

If you allow it, Daftar reads your phone's location only while the app is open, to fill in "I'm here" for a place and to show your distance to saved places. Your location is used on the device and is not sent to us. You can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode, snap a photo of a menu or place, or attach a photo. Text recognition from photos runs on your device. Photos stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

## Biometrics and screen lock

If you turn on app lock, Daftar asks Android to verify your fingerprint, face, or screen lock. Daftar never sees or stores your biometric data.

## Services that need the internet

Daftar works offline. A few optional features contact outside services, and only when you use them:

- **Google Maps.** The map screen loads map tiles from Google Maps. Google receives the information any map request involves (such as your IP address and the area being viewed) and handles it under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Address search.** When you search for an address, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places. See https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
- **Open Food Facts.** When you scan a product barcode to look it up, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to get the product name and details. No personal information is sent. See https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
- **Links.** Opening a phone number, directions, or a web link hands it to another app on your phone, which has its own policy.

We do not control these services. If you never use these features, Daftar makes no network requests for them.

## Children

Daftar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
